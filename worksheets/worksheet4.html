🎯 Part 1: My SMART Savings Goals

Set two savings goals — one short-term and one long-term — using the SMART framework.

Short-Term Goal (within 6 months)
Specific: What exactly do I want?
Measurable: How much?
Achievable: How much can I save/month?
Time-Bound: By what date?
Long-Term Goal (1 year or more)
Specific: What exactly do I want?
Measurable: How much?
Achievable: How much can I save/month?
Time-Bound: By what date?

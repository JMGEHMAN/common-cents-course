💡 Parent Tips

  • 🗣️ Ask your child to explain each session in their own words after completing it
  • 🏪 Connect lessons to real-life experiences (grocery shopping, bank visits)
  • 💰 Consider a matching savings program — you match what they save each month
  • 🎮 Let them make small money mistakes while the stakes are low
  • 📱 Show them your actual budget (simplified) to make it real

🏆 Milestones to Celebrate

  • ✅ First session completed — give a high five!
  • 🌟 First quiz score above 80% — celebrate the learning!
  • 💰 5 sessions done — halfway hero award!
  • 🎓 All 10 sessions + quizzes — graduation ceremony!
  • 📝 All worksheets completed — extra recognition!

📚 Discussion Questions

  • 💬 "What's one thing you want to save for right now?"
  • 💬 "Can you help me figure out if this purchase is a need or want?"
  • 💬 "What would you do if you won $1,000?"
  • 💬 "What job would you like to have someday?"
  • 💬 "What does being 'good with money' mean to you?"

The Curriculum

10 Sessions to Money Mastery 🎓

Each session takes 30–45 minutes and includes a lesson, activity, and quiz.

1

What Is Money?

The history & purpose of money

Explore where money came from, why we use it, and the different forms money takes — from coins to digital payments.

🪙 History 💳 Currency
⏱ 30 min · Beginner Start →
2

Needs vs. Wants

Making smart spending decisions

Learn to tell the difference between things you need to survive and things you simply want — a key skill for smart spending.

🛒 Spending 🧠 Decisions
⏱ 35 min · Beginner Start →
3

Budgeting Basics

Plan where every dollar goes

Create your first budget! Learn the 50/30/20 rule and practice allocating income to needs, wants, and savings.

📊 Budget 🧮 Math
⏱ 40 min · Beginner Start →
4

Saving & Goals

Turn dreams into savings plans

Set SMART savings goals, understand the power of compound interest, and learn why paying yourself first matters.

🎯 Goals 📈 Growth
⏱ 40 min · Intermediate Start →
5

Banking 101

How banks work & keeping money safe

Discover what banks do, the difference between checking and savings accounts, and how FDIC insurance protects your money.

🏦 Banking 🔐 Safety
⏱ 35 min · Intermediate Start →
6

Credit & Debt

Borrowing money responsibly

Understand how credit cards, loans, and interest work — and why good credit habits now set you up for a strong financial future.

💳 Credit ⚖️ Debt
⏱ 40 min · Intermediate Start →
7

Earning Money

Jobs, income, and entrepreneurship

Explore the many ways to earn money — jobs, side hustles, entrepreneurship — and discover how taxes affect your paycheck.

💼 Jobs 🚀 Entrepreneurship
⏱ 35 min · Intermediate Start →
8

Investing Intro

Make your money work for you

Learn the basics of stocks, bonds, and mutual funds — and why starting to invest young is one of the best money moves you can make.

📈 Investing ⏳ Long-term
⏱ 45 min · Advanced Start →
9

Smart Shopping

Getting the best value for money

Master comparison shopping, avoid marketing tricks, and learn to resist impulse buys so your money stretches further.

🛍️ Shopping 🔍 Research
⏱ 35 min · Intermediate Start →
10

Money Mindset

Attitudes & habits for lasting success

Build the mindset of financial winners — gratitude, generosity, delayed gratification, and a long-term view of wealth.

🌱 Mindset 🎓 Graduation
⏱ 40 min · All Levels Start →

📖 What Is a Budget?

A Budget Is a Plan for Your Money

A budget tells your money where to go BEFORE you spend it, instead of wondering where it went AFTER. It's not about restricting fun — it's about making sure you have money for what matters most.

💡 Why Budgeting Is a Superpower

People who budget save 15-20% more money than those who don't — not because they earn more, but because they make intentional choices. A budget gives you control, reduces money stress, and helps you reach your goals faster.

⚡ The 50/30/20 Rule

One of the most popular budgeting frameworks divides your income into three buckets:

50%
Needs (50%)
Essentials: housing, food, utilities, transportation, healthcare. These come first.
30%
Wants (30%)
Fun things: entertainment, dining out, shopping, hobbies. Important for enjoyment!
20%
Savings & Debt (20%)
Future security: savings, emergency fund, paying off debt, investing.

📉 When the Budget Doesn't Balance

⚠️ Spending More Than You Earn = Deficit

If your total spending is more than your income, you have a budget deficit. This leads to debt. The fix: either earn more or spend less — or both!

✂️
Cut wants first — Reduce entertainment, dining out, subscriptions
🔍
Find cheaper alternatives — Cook at home, use free apps instead of paid ones
💼
Earn more — Find ways to bring in extra income

Needs vs. Wants 🛒

Learn the most important money distinction there is — and why it changes every spending decision you'll ever make.

🎯 What You'll Learn

1
The difference between needs and wants
2
Why this distinction is critical for smart spending
3
How advertisers blur the line between needs and wants
4
How to make better spending decisions

📖 Needs: The Essentials

What Is a Need?

A need is something you must have to survive and function — things that, without them, your health, safety, or ability to work/learn would be seriously affected.

🏠
Shelter — A safe place to live (housing, heat in winter)
🍎
Food & Water — Nutritious meals and clean drinking water
👕
Basic Clothing — Clothes appropriate for weather and school
🏥
Healthcare — Medical care and medicine when sick
📚
Education — Learning and school supplies

🌟 Wants: The Nice-to-Haves

What Is a Want?

A want is something that improves your quality of life or brings enjoyment, but you can live without it. Wants aren't bad — they make life fun! The key is making sure needs are covered first.

🎮
Video games — Fun, but not essential for survival
👟
Brand-name sneakers — Shoes are a need; expensive brand-names are a want
🍕
Eating out — Food is a need; restaurant meals are usually a want
📱
Latest phone — Communication can be a need; the newest model is a want
🎬
Streaming subscriptions — Entertainment wants

⚠️ The Gray Area: It's Not Always Clear!

⚠️ Needs Can Become Wants

Context matters a lot! Food is a need — but a $50 restaurant dinner is a want. Clothes are a need — but 20 pairs of shoes is a want. Transportation can be a need — but a luxury car is a want. Always ask: "What's the minimum I actually need here?"

🛑 The Advertiser's Trick

Companies spend billions of dollars convincing you that their products are needs when they're really wants. Watch for words like "essential," "must-have," "you need this," or peer pressure from friends. Recognizing these tactics is a financial superpower!

💡 The 24-Hour Rule

Before You Buy — Wait!

For any non-essential purchase, wait 24 hours before buying. This simple trick eliminates most impulse purchases. After sleeping on it, you'll often realize you didn't really want it that badly — and you just saved money!

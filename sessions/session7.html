📖 Types of Income

⏰
Active Income (Earned)
Money you earn by working — a job, freelancing, babysitting. You trade time for money. When you stop working, the money stops.
😴
Passive Income
Money that keeps coming in even when you're not working — rental income, dividends from stocks, royalties, a YouTube channel. The goal of the wealthy!
💰
Portfolio Income
Money from investments — selling stocks for a profit, capital gains. Requires upfront investment of money.

🧾 Understanding Your Paycheck

Gross Pay vs. Net Pay

Gross Pay = what you earn before deductions. Net Pay (take-home pay) = what you actually receive after taxes and other deductions. The difference can be 20-30% or more!

⚠️ Taxes Aren't Optional

The government takes a portion of your income for taxes that fund roads, schools, police, and other services. Federal income tax, state tax, Social Security, and Medicare are automatically deducted from most paychecks.

🚀 Ways Teens Can Earn Money

👶
Babysitting / Pet Sitting: Great starting point — flexible hours, good pay.
✂️
Lawn Care / Snow Shoveling: Seasonal but can earn $20–$50 per job.
💻
Online Freelancing: Graphic design, tutoring, social media — sell your skills online.
🎨
Sell Crafts / Art: Etsy, local markets — turn creativity into income.
📦
Reselling: Buy items at thrift stores or garage sales, sell online for profit.
📱
Content Creation: YouTube, TikTok, blogs — build an audience and monetize.

🏢 Entrepreneurship 101

Being Your Own Boss

An entrepreneur creates a business to solve a problem or fill a need. The rewards can be greater than a regular job, but so are the risks. Successful entrepreneurs are creative, persistent, willing to learn from failure, and great at managing money.

💡 Start Simple

The best first business solves a problem you've noticed in your neighborhood. Ask: "What do people around me need that isn't being provided well?" Start small, learn as you go, and grow from there.

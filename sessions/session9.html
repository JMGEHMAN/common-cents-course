📖 Price vs. Value

Cheapest Isn't Always Best

Price is what you pay. Value is what you get. A $30 pair of shoes that lasts 3 years is better value than a $10 pair that falls apart in 3 months. Smart shoppers think about cost-per-use, durability, and quality — not just sticker price.

💡 Cost Per Use Formula

Divide the price by how many times you'll use it. A $60 jacket worn 100 times = $0.60 per use. A $15 trendy top worn twice = $7.50 per use. The expensive one is actually the better deal!

🎭 Marketing Tricks to Watch Out For

⏰
False Urgency ("Limited Time!")
Creates panic buying. Most "limited time" sales just repeat next week.
💯
Anchoring ("Was $100, Now $60!")
The "was" price is often inflated to make the sale seem bigger.
📦
Bundling ("Buy 3 Get 1 Free!")
You spend more total. Only a deal if you needed all the items.
😍
Social Proof ("Everyone's Buying It!")
Popularity doesn't mean it's the best or right for YOU.
🎁
Free Shipping Thresholds
"Free shipping on orders over $50!" — you spend $20 extra to save $5.

🔍 Smart Shopping Strategies

📋
Make a List: Shop with a list and stick to it. Never shop hungry or bored.
🔄
Compare Prices: Check multiple stores and websites before buying anything over $20.
⭐
Read Reviews: Real customer reviews reveal quality issues marketing glosses over.
⏳
Wait for Sales: Major sales happen on Black Friday, end of season, and holidays.
✂️
Use Coupons & Cashback: Apps like Honey, Rakuten, and Ibotta save money automatically.
🏪
Consider Generic Brands: Store-brand products are often identical to name brands at 30-50% less.

⚡ Stopping Impulse Buys

The 3-Question Rule

Before any unplanned purchase, ask yourself: 1) Did I plan to buy this? 2) Do I need it or just want it right now? 3) Can I afford it without hurting my savings goals? If you can't answer "yes" to all three, put it back and wait 24 hours.

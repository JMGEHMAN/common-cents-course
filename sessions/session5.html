📖 What Do Banks Do?

Banks Are Money Middlemen

A bank takes deposits from people who save money and lends that money to people who need it (for homes, cars, businesses). The bank pays you interest for using your money, and charges borrowers higher interest — that's how banks make a profit.

🔐
Safe Storage: Banks keep your money much safer than a piggy bank — insured up to $250,000!
💳
Convenient Access: ATMs, debit cards, apps — get your money anytime, anywhere.
📈
Earn Interest: Your savings actually grow while sitting in the bank.
🏠
Lending: Banks provide loans for big purchases like cars and homes.

⚖️ Checking vs. Savings Accounts

💳
Checking Account
For everyday spending. Comes with a debit card and checks. Easy to access. Usually earns little or no interest. Think of it as your "spending wallet."
🏦
Savings Account
For money you're saving. Earns interest. Usually has limits on how often you can withdraw. Think of it as your "future wallet."

💡 High-Yield Savings Accounts

Online banks often offer High-Yield Savings Accounts with interest rates 10–20x higher than traditional banks! If you're saving money, look for the highest interest rate you can find.

🛡️ FDIC Insurance — Your Safety Net

What Is FDIC Insurance?

The Federal Deposit Insurance Corporation (FDIC) insures bank deposits up to $250,000 per account type, per bank. This means even if a bank fails (goes out of business), the government guarantees your money back. This is why banks are much safer than keeping cash at home!

⚠️ Always Choose FDIC-Insured Banks

When opening a bank account, look for the FDIC logo. Credit unions have similar protection through NCUA. Avoid uninsured "banks" or apps that don't have this protection.

📱 Online Banking Safety Tips

🔑
Use a strong, unique password and never share it with anyone.
📲
Enable two-factor authentication (2FA) for extra security.
🔒
Only access your bank on secure, private Wi-Fi — never public hotspots.
🚨
Never click links in emails claiming to be your bank — go directly to the website.
📊
Check your statements regularly to catch any unauthorized charges quickly.
